About Us

Meet Your Coach – Jaskiran Kaur

"At Sky High Institute, we help students and professionals improve spoken English, confidence, interview skills, and personality development — because communication is the bridge to every opportunity."

With over 20 years of experience in teaching, coaching, and student development, Jaskiran Kaur is the Founder and Director of Sky High Institute in Punjab, India — a leading hub for IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, and Spoken English training. She is also a former Lecturer at BELA College and Ex-Research Scholar at NIS, bringing academic rigor and deep empathy to every session.

  • Personally taught and mentored over 7000+ students across India and globally
  • Recognized by IDP at the 8th Annual Partners Recognition Meet
  • Awarded by British Council for Excellence Performance 2019-20
  • Multiple branch network: Morinda, Sri Chamkaur Sahib & Online
  • PhD research in Sport Sciences – brings a scientific approach to learning
  • Services available in-person (Punjab) and online globally
